# 36.1 The Fibonacci Congruum and the $\mathcal{N}=4$ Supercharge

$$
\boxed{\; h(m,n) := \mathrm{Im}\bigl((m+ni)^4\bigr) = 4mn(m+n)(m-n) = 4mn(m^2-n^2) \;}
$$

### Exposition
A "congruum" (defined by Fibonacci in 1225 CE) is the difference between areas of nested squares in a rational right triangle problem. The congruum $h(m,n)$ operates structurally as the explicit evaluation of this $\mathcal{N}=4$ supercharge operator on the left-moving string sector.

### Derivation

**Step 1:** Let the state in the Ramond lattice be parametrized by the pair of integers $(m,n)$, corresponding to the complex number $q = m + ni$.
**Step 2:** Compute the 4th power of $q$ using the binomial theorem:
$$
q^4 = (m + ni)^4 = m^4 + 4m^3(ni) + 6m^2(ni)^2 + 4m(ni)^3 + (ni)^4

**Step 3:** Substitute $i^2 = -1$, $i^3 = -i$, and $i^4 = 1$:

**Step 5:** Factor out the common term $4mn$ and apply the difference of squares:

This precisely matches the classical definition of the congruum $h(m,n)$.
