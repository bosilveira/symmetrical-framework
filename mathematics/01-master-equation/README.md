# 01 — The second-order Master and the Classification Theorem

**Primary mathematical status:** the second-order nonlinear Cauchy–Euler equation and its global real finite-norm classification. This layer is **before** Sturm–Liouville, variational, Ermakov–Pinney, Riccati spectral theory and applications, even though these provide interesting related interpretations.

Read [operator and conserved energy](01-01-operator-energy.md), [Classification Theorems 4.1–4.2](01-02-classification.md), then [general levels, Theorem 4.5](01-03-general-levels.md).

## Fundamental distinction

- A specified hyperbolic-secant kernel is **one selected solution**.
- The second-order Master is a **differential equation** with many additional real solutions (constant, periodic, sign-changing).
- Its first integral is an energy **at any level**, not necessarily zero.
- The first-order squared constraint describes the zero-energy locus, but alone admits spurious/insufficiently regular solutions.
- Only the **globality + regularity + finite weighted norm** hypotheses select the translated signed sech family.
