# 07 — Applications and further constructed layers

**Status:** deliberate outermost layer. These topics may be mathematically interesting or yield useful models, but they are **not** prerequisites for the original function, Master classification, integral transforms, probability law, or selected local differential-geometry dictionary.

Examples of future, separately reviewed modules include: mathematical physics with a specified PDE/action, geometric and spectral models with fixed operators, modular/arithmetic and number-theoretic constructions, global surface period problems, and other domain-specific uses.

Every candidate must explicitly provide:

1. Its **independently chosen structures** (ambient manifold, operator, gauge group, modular form, distribution, dynamical boundary data, etc.).
2. The exact **map from the foundations** with complete variable and parameter binding.
3. A proof of each proposed invariant, implication or equivalence, including global and local scope.
4. Correct citations to established outside results and evidence for any novelty claim.
5. A status in the canonical claim registry, with failed cases and errata preserved.

The historical [moonshine/K3 and phenomenology archive](../../archive/legacy-interpretations/README.md) remains archived. Coincident integers, similar ODEs or familiar special-function factors do not establish identifications of K3 surfaces, sporadic-group modules, Calabi–Yau manifolds, physical particles or gauge fields.

**Promotion rule:** a source chapter enters applications only after its required foundation and secondary-theory contracts can be read without guessing the meanings of its symbols. The original research material remains available in [research](../../research/README.md).
