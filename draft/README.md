# Draft investigations

This directory preserves **complete, provisional mathematical investigations** before claim-by-claim review and incorporation into the Symmetrical Framework's four canonical layers. Its contents are research records, **not automatically accepted theorems**.

## Numbering and titles

- Use `000 Objective Mathematical Title.md`, `010 Next Investigation.md`, `020 Another Investigation.md`, and so on (increments of ten leave room for intermediate entries).
- Allocate each number once. Keep earlier drafts in place when consolidating; mark them as incorporated or superseded rather than silently replacing their history.
- Use objective titles identifying the mathematics, not a claim of novelty.

## Suggested contents of each draft

1. Canonical definitions and parameter domains (`r`, `c`, `mu`, etc.).
2. Motivation and links to source claim IDs or earlier investigations.
3. Exact identities, derivations, proofs, and exceptional cases.
4. Independent symbolic/numeric checks and reproducibility information.
5. Corrections, open points, and the boundary between known mathematics and possible new consequences.
6. Proposed destinations in Layers I–IV, without editing those layers prematurely.

Maintain distinct evidence labels: **proved in this draft**, **checked computationally**, **known/classical**, **conjectural**, and **not yet independently audited**.

## GitHub Markdown conventions

- Inline mathematics: ``$`x`$`` (backticks immediately inside the dollar signs).
- Display mathematics: unindented fenced `math` blocks, with one blank line immediately above and below the fence; keep each expression on one line.
- Avoid `\boxed`, `\tag`, and `\operatorname`; prefer `\mathrm{sech}` for the hyperbolic secant.
- Keep the original stepwise calculations visible, not just final results.

## Consolidation

A draft does not become canonical merely by being saved here. Audit its assertions against the research ledger, verify domains, compare with the literature, assign stable theorem and claim references, then incorporate each result into its **authoritative mathematical section**. Preserve references back to the originating draft.
