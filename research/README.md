# Research Materials — Provisional and Historical Investigations

This folder preserves **mathematical research before complete theorem-by-theorem review**. It is not a second canonical mathematical reference. In particular, some historical source files contain valid elementary identities followed by non sequiturs, unwarranted physical interpretations, unsupported equivalences, or overconfident verification language.

## Collections

- [Active mathematical investigations](active-drafts/README.md): the detailed numbered research sequence from the earlier default branch, retained intact with its provisional status and internal index.
- [Legacy eight-part materials](legacy-parts/README.md): Parts II–VI and VIII from the old encyclopedic branch, preserved for extraction, review and possible promotion.
- [Older identity/theorem catalogs](legacy-catalog/README.md): exploratory broad catalogs, skeletal theorem files and conversational materials. Their global assertions are **not** adopted by the reference.
- [Archived legacy interpretations](../archive/README.md): the old Part VII moonshine/K3/phenomenology track, deliberately removed from the canonical reading path.

## Rules for using these sources

1. Do not quote a result as proved merely because its historical prose uses “Theorem”, “naturally”, “isomorphic”, “the framework proves”, or “exact”.
2. Separate the algebraic identity from the independently chosen geometric, spectral, arithmetic or physical interpretation. A shared $`\mathrm{sech}`$ formula or matching integer is not an isomorphism.
3. Check the coordinate, scale $`c`$, resolution $`r`$, amplitude normalization, energy constant, domain and branch restrictions against the [canonical notation](../reference/NOTATION.md).
4. Consult the [claim registry](../reference/CLAIM_REGISTRY.md) and exact proofs in [reference](../reference/README.md). Record any contradiction, counterexample and repair.
5. Promote a claim to the reference only with an explicit statement, complete proof or cited known theorem, reversible map when an equivalence is claimed, and a stable ID.

All original substantive files were **moved by their exact Git blobs**, not paraphrased. The previous branch tips remain available as immutable provenance points in [MIGRATION.md](../MIGRATION.md).
