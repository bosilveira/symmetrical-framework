# Research Archive — Historical and Superseded Material

This area preserves **source material that is not part of the canonical mathematics**. It remains readable so that previously explored ideas, calculations and errors can be traced.

The historical [Gemini conversational sources](conversation-sources/README.md) from Part I are also preserved here as unreviewed originals; links remain at their previous locations. They are useful for reconstructing the research history, not independent evidence for a claim.

The entire former **Part VII — Moonshine, K3, and Equivariant Geometry** is now in [legacy interpretations](legacy-interpretations/README.md), with the exact original content unchanged. It includes sweeping claims that have not been established by the framework's defining kernel and in several cases require independent mathematical or physical constructions.

**Important distinctions:** a known theorem about a Leech lattice, Mathieu group, K3 surface or elliptic curve is not automatically a theorem *about the Symmetrical Framework*. A formula producing a familiar special value, modular invariant or integer does not identify the associated geometric object or representation. A scalar expression does not construct a gauge theory or particle spectrum.

Potentially useful calculations can be reviewed individually and promoted to [reference](../reference/README.md) with correct hypotheses and proofs. The archive status is **not** a claim that every line is false; it is a clear warning that the compiled implications are not validated.

The provenance snapshot and location changes are recorded in [MIGRATION.md](../MIGRATION.md).
