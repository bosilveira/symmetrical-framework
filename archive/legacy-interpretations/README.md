## Why this archive exists

The collection conflates sound facts from different mathematical theories with unproved identifications. Examples found in its sources include: equating Mellin poles with Atiyah–Bott fixed points; treating two geometric circles as proving separate K3 cohomological obstructions; asserting a secant residue pattern forces Mathieu twining data; proposing a direct bridge from Fibonacci congrua to \`M24\` representations; and inferring quantum entanglement, a BKT phase, or precisely three particle generations from a selected elliptic fibration. These implications **do not follow from the displayed kernel alone**.

Some statements about special functions, lattices, conics, elliptic integrals and classical modular forms may still be correct in isolation. To reuse any one of them, first:

1. State the exact definitions and domain, including the parameters and independent variables.
2. Identify and prove the map into the named outside mathematical structure.
3. Check that an asserted equivalence has a converse and preserves the relevant invariant.
4. Cite established external literature where applicable; do not cite the historical prose as its own proof.
5. Place an independently verified result into [Claim Registry](../../reference/CLAIM_REGISTRY.md) and preserve the archival original.

No material has been rewritten or deleted inside these nine historical files.
