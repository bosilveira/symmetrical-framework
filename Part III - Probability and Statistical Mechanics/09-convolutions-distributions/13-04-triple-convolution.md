# 13.4 Three-Fold Multiplicative Convolution

The multiplicative autoconvolution can be extended to three functions. Remarkably, the resulting analytic form loses the hyperbolic sine denominator of the two-fold convolution and returns to a hyperbolic secant form, now modulated by a quadratic polynomial in the logarithmic coordinate.

$$
\boxed{\; (f *_{M} f *_{M} f)(x; r, c) = \frac{\pi^2 + 4r^2 \log^2(x/c^3)}{16r^2 x} \operatorname{sech}\left(r\log\left(\frac{x}{c^3}\right)\right) \;}
$$

### Exposition

This result demonstrates the recursive algebraic structure generated by the Mellin convolution. While successive spatial convolutions become increasingly complex to calculate directly, evaluating them in the transform domain using the convolution theorem reveals that the fundamental $\operatorname{sech}$ structure is preserved and modulated by polynomials. 

### Derivation

The derivation utilizes the convolution theorem in the Mellin/Fourier domain, coupled with a fundamental algebraic identity for the derivatives of the hyperbolic secant.

**Step 1: Transform Space Definition**

By the Mellin convolution theorem, the Mellin transform of a three-fold multiplicative convolution is the cube of the individual Mellin transforms:

$$
\mathcal{M}\{f *_M f *_M f\}(s) = \bigl[ \mathcal{M}\{f\}(s) \bigr]^3
$$

In the canonical logarithmic coordinate system ($u = \log(x/c^3)$), this is equivalent to finding the inverse Fourier transform of the cube of the characteristic function: $\propto \operatorname{sech}^3\left(\frac{\pi\omega}{2r}\right)$.

**Step 2: The Hyperbolic Derivative Identity**

We bypass complex integration by using an elementary identity linking $\operatorname{sech}^3(z)$ to its second derivative. Differentiating $\operatorname{sech}(z)$ twice gives:

$$
\operatorname{sech}'(z) = -\operatorname{sech}(z)\tanh(z)
$$

$$
\operatorname{sech}''(z) = -\operatorname{sech}(z)(\operatorname{sech}^2(z)) + (\tanh(z))\operatorname{sech}(z)\tanh(z) = \operatorname{sech}(z)(\tanh^2(z) - \operatorname{sech}^2(z))
$$

Using $\tanh^2(z) = 1 - \operatorname{sech}^2(z)$, this becomes $\operatorname{sech}''(z) = \operatorname{sech}(z)(1 - 2\operatorname{sech}^2(z))$. Rearranging yields the vital identity:

$$
2\operatorname{sech}^3(z) = \operatorname{sech}(z) - \operatorname{sech}''(z)
$$

**Step 3: Apply Inverse Fourier Transform**

Let $G(\omega) = \operatorname{sech}\left(\frac{\pi\omega}{2r}\right)$. Its inverse Fourier transform generates the log-space density: $\mathcal{F}^{-1}\{G\}(u) = \frac{r}{\pi}\operatorname{sech}(ru)$.

Applying our identity with $z = \frac{\pi\omega}{2r}$:

$$
2 G(\omega)^3 = G(\omega) - \left(\frac{2r}{\pi}\right)^2 G''(\omega)
$$

Using the Fourier property $\mathcal{F}^{-1}\{G''(\omega)\} = -u^2 \mathcal{F}^{-1}\{G(\omega)\}$:

$$
2 \mathcal{F}^{-1}\{G^3\} = \frac{r}{\pi}\operatorname{sech}(ru) - \left(\frac{2r}{\pi}\right)^2 \left( -u^2 \frac{r}{\pi}\operatorname{sech}(ru) \right)
$$

$$
= \frac{r}{\pi}\operatorname{sech}(ru) \left[ 1 + \frac{4r^2 u^2}{\pi^2} \right] = \frac{r(\pi^2 + 4r^2 u^2)}{\pi^3} \operatorname{sech}(ru)
$$

**Step 4: Track Prefactors to recover the $f$-Convolution**

The integral of $f$ is $\pi/(2r)$. The triple convolution must integrate to $(\pi/(2r))^3 = \pi^3 / (8r^3)$.

We multiply the density by this total mass, and push forward to $x$-space by dividing by the Jacobian $x$, with $u = \log(x/c^3)$:

$$
(f *_M f *_M f)(x) = \frac{\pi^3}{8r^3} \cdot \frac{r(\pi^2 + 4r^2 \log^2(x/c^3))}{2\pi^3 x} \operatorname{sech}\left(r\log\frac{x}{c^3}\right)
$$

$$
= \frac{\pi^2 + 4r^2 \log^2(x/c^3)}{16r^2 x} \operatorname{sech}\left(r\log\frac{x}{c^3}\right)
$$

This completes the derivation.
