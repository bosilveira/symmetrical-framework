# Chapter 2 — Verification, counterexamples, and scope

**Current mathematical register**, replacing informal draft commentary.

- **[E]** Inversion: $f(c^2/x)=x^2f(x)/c^2$ and $f(c^2/x)\,d(c^2/x)=-f(x)\,dx$. The older $f\,dx/x$ inversion assertion is false.
- **[E]** Joint homogeneity: $(x\partial_x+c\partial_c)f=-f$, at fixed $r$.
- **[E]** Reciprocity of laws: $1/X\sim\rho(\cdot;r,1/c)$; central inversion $c^2/X$ preserves $\rho(\cdot;r,c)$.
- **[E]** Half-Cauchy map and arcsine logistic coordinate with correct Jacobians.
- **[E]** Two- and three-scale hyperbolic product formulas.
- **[E]** Distinct spatial and logarithmic superpotentials, with explicit factorization for the latter.
- **[E]** Restricted CDF angle-addition and global multiplicative group law, with branch/domain conditions.

**Limits of inference.** A simple identity is not a physical interaction or a group action on a new geometric space without an explicit map. Inversion plus scaling alone do not uniquely specify $f$; the classification theorem and its weighted norm are derived in Chapter 3 and in the separate foundational manuscript.
