$$

$$

**Step 3: Evaluate the Complex Hessian**
We compute the mixed partial derivative $\partial_z \partial_{\bar{z}} \mathcal{K}$:
*   First derivative with respect to $z$:
    $$
    \frac{\partial \mathcal{K}}{\partial z} = \frac{2r e^{2r(z + \bar{z})}}{1 + e^{2r(z + \bar{z})}}
    $$
*   Second derivative with respect to $\bar{z}$:
    $$

    $$

    $$

To recover the physical distance along the real spatial axis, we restrict the complex coordinates back to the real slice: $z = \bar{z} = u$.
$$
g_{uu} = \frac{ 4r^2 e^{2r(u + u)} }{ (1 + e^{2r(u + u)})^2 } = \frac{ 4r^2 e^{4ru} }{ (1 + e^{4ru})^2 }
