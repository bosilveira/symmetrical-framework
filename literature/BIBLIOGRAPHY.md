## Statements recalled from memory (not checked; locate sources before citing)

- Fowler / Delaunay-type solutions: periodic positive solutions of the critical equation on R^n minus {0}.
- Lie-Scheffers theorem, Vessiot-Guldberg algebras, nonlinear superposition (Riccati, Ermakov-Pinney as sl(2) Lie systems); candidate authors to search: Lie and Scheffers; Anderson, Harnad, Winternitz; Carinena, de Lucas and collaborators.
- Standard (continuous q-ultraspherical) Rogers recurrence; Meixner-Pollaczek polynomials.
- Thomas-Wigner rotation equals the area of the hyperbolic right triangle; Kostant-Souriau prequantization of the Hopf bundle; geodesic flow on the unit tangent bundle of the hyperbolic plane as right multiplication by the diagonal subgroup.
- Milnor's classification of three-dimensional unimodular Lie groups by the signs of the cyclic structure constants.
- McGuire's exact solution of the attractive one-dimensional delta Bose gas (energies and the mean-field soliton scaling).
- Chowla-Selberg / Ramanujan evaluations at CM points of the lattice sums.
